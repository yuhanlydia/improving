## 3. Figure data

All five figures are generated by `rewrite/make_figures.py` from the sources above. Every data
constant in that file was machine-compared against `EVAL64` / `CMP64` / `PER16` / `REPORT`:
`CM4_BASE`, `CM4`, `P1_R5`, `CM4_DELTA_SPD`, `CM4_DELTA_BASE`, `CM4_DELTA_BASE_ALL`, `EVAL64`,

differences to the paired `CMP64` differences its own caption declares).

## 4. The adverse result — declared, not buried

The frozen protocol declares success as **both** a positive paired ΔD₄ **and** a pass@1 difference
whose interval lower bound is ≥ −1 pp, against **both** comparators.

| Comparator | ΔD₄ | pass@1 | Criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPD-hard (16-tier, r5) | +0.293 [0.233, 0.353] ✔ | −0.0118 [−0.0190, −0.0048] ✘ | **not met** |
| plain (64-tier) | +0.295 [0.249, 0.343] ✔ | −1.12 [−1.61, −0.63] ✘ | **not met** |
| undistilled (16-tier, r5) | −0.315 [−0.394, −0.240] ✘ | +0.0225 [0.0120, 0.0335] ✔ | **not met** |

Placement: stated once plainly in §5.4, once in §6 (Limitations), and in the abstract as
"It gives up 1.4 pp of pass@1 against hard projection; we report that cost rather than hide it."
Never called a "trade-off" and never called "noninferior" — the interval excludes zero on the wrong
side. Full protocol and per-comparison table in Appendix A.

## 5. Explicitly NOT claimed (and why)

| Not claimed | Why |
|---|---|
| Algorithm / semantic diversity | `strategy_annotation_status` is empty in every record. All coverage statements are about **AST implementation-structure classes**, a proxy. Blinded algorithm annotation was designed but not performed. |
| Seed-level uncertainty | One training seed (43). The five rounds are sequential, not replicates. No seed-level interval exists and none is drawn. |
| A mechanism proof | The matched-blend / random-eigenvector / isotropic controls are implemented but **not run**. §3.3 says this in the text. |
| Transfer or generality | Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B-Instruct and MBPP only. No HumanEval+/EvalPlus, no second benchmark, no second model. |
| Accuracy superiority over the comparators | It does not hold: −1.12 pp pass@1 vs plain, −1.43 pp vs SPD-hard. |
| That the paired bootstrap was re-run here | The shipped bundle contains hashes only, not the per-task jsonl. The intervals are reported as stored, not recomputed. |
